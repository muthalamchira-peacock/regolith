Training dataset

Every decision you make is recorded as structured data.

Data card

Records (one JSON per line): outpost (allocation, state, crew actions, outcome per sol), eva (rover pose, tilt, battery, throttle, steer at 4 Hz on procedural lunar-style terrain), hazard_tag (player label vs ground truth), selfright (lean side, limb choice, success), eva_end. Anonymous, no personal data. Use: imitation learning for rover navigation, power-aware route cost models, human risk-preference data, and planning benchmarks for resource-allocation agents. Limits: the physics is a simplified model, so it supports high-level policy and perception pre-training, not low-level servo control, until the sim is matched to the real rover’s kinematics and IMU noise. Resource figures are simplified from NASA ISS life-support, Curiosity RAD and lunar dose data.
